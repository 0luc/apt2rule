threat2apt

Threat assessment → ATT&CK groups → apt2rule

  • MITRE ATT&CK®
  • Evidence for every pick
  • Runs in your browser

Tick the countries your threat assessment names, where your organisation is and what sector it is in. threat2apt searches every ATT&CK group and campaign description and suggests the groups that match, with the sentences that prove it. You decide, then send your picks to apt2rule. Nothing you tick leaves your browser.

Disclaimer. This product is fully vibe coded (built with AI assistance) and comes with no guarantees. Attribution is read from ATT&CK's description text and can be wrong or missing: check the evidence.

01 Threat countries

The countries whose state actors your threat assessment names. Groups attributed to them match on country.

    Only countries ATT&CK groups are attributed to are listed. The number is how many groups.

    02 Your area

    Where your organisation is, e.g. Europe and your country. Groups whose descriptions name a place in your area are ticked by default; groups that only name places elsewhere sink to the bottom. Groups naming no place stay in between.

    03 Your sector

    What your organisation is. A group matches when its or its campaigns' descriptions name one of the terms.

    Whole words; terms in capitals (EU, US, NATO) match case-sensitively.

    04 Show

    Tiers

    Last seen comes from a group's campaigns. Most groups have none: their last seen is unknown and they stay visible unless you tick “also hide unknown”.
